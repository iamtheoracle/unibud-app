# UNIBUD Editorial, Story and Media Standard

## Non-negotiable rule

Discovery is not publication. No story, campus notice, scholarship, deadline, trend, image, video or opportunity may be surfaced as verified/current merely because an agent found it or an AI summarized it.

## Agent responsibilities

- **Bud** is the only user-facing assistant and explains what is known, unknown and next.
- **Spark** coordinates only the specialists needed for the task.
- **Oracle** researches, cross-checks claims, identifies conflicts and preserves source provenance.
- **Browser** retrieves real content through a connected, permitted provider. If no provider exists, it must return unavailable, never demo results.
- **Pulse** may identify current activity only from real, timestamped inputs.
- **Community** checks campus/community relevance and context.
- **Vision** inspects visual material for relevance, manipulation signals and mismatch; it cannot establish image ownership or licensing.
- **Creator/Orbit** may prepare layouts, crops, captions and visual drafts. They cannot grant publication rights.
- **Guardian** reviews scams, impersonation, privacy, harassment, harmful claims and unsafe calls to action.
- **Editorial review** validates provenance, dates, claims, media rights, presentation and audience before approval.

## Required source record

Every candidate must retain: original URL, publisher/author, original publication time, retrieval time, exact quoted/extracted evidence, claim-to-source mapping, jurisdiction/campus scope, freshness expiry, conflict notes, reviewer and decision history.

Prefer primary sources for official notices: university registrar/admissions/faculty domains, official government agencies, scholarship administrators and original event organizers. Secondary reporting may add context but must not replace the primary record for consequential dates, eligibility or policy.

A social post, screenshot, forwarded message, unattributed PDF or search snippet is a lead, not proof. Cross-check consequential claims against a second independent source where possible. If primary sources conflict, disclose the conflict and hold publication pending review.

## Image and media rights

For each image retain its original source page, creator, exact license or written permission, required attribution, retrieval time and any restrictions. A public URL does not mean an image is free to reuse. Do not hotlink or republish copyrighted media without permission. Prefer original UNIBUD artwork, public-domain material, or assets with an explicit compatible license. Preserve attribution and license notices. If rights are unclear, omit the image and use a designed text card or an approved original visual.

## Editorial gate

1. Discover candidate; store original source unchanged.
2. Parse claims, dates, named entities, audience, location and media metadata.
3. Oracle verifies claims against primary sources and records evidence.
4. Browser confirms the source is reachable and the canonical page matches the candidate.
5. Guardian checks fraud, impersonation, privacy, safety and manipulation risks.
6. Vision checks image/context mismatch; a human checks rights.
7. Editorial specialist checks clarity, neutrality, accessibility, campus relevance and duplication.
8. Human editor approves, rejects or requests changes. Approval is recorded with reviewer and timestamp.
9. Only approved, unexpired records can be published. Expired or disputed content is withdrawn or visibly marked.
10. Corrections are versioned; do not silently rewrite source facts.

## Publication rules

- No invented timestamps, engagement, quotations, names, sources, image credits or verification badges.
- No AI-only auto-publication of consequential educational notices, scholarships, exam dates, financial opportunities or safety alerts.
- Never claim that an image is authentic or licensed based only on visual analysis.
- Never treat the AI's confidence as evidence.
- Keep original facts separate from AI summaries, interpretation and recommendations.
- Display source, publication date, last checked, relevant audience and expiry where applicable.
- If source freshness cannot be established, say "date unverified" and do not label it current.
- Corrections and takedowns must propagate to feed cards, search, notifications and cached previews.

## Current application boundary

The current browsing boundary has no connected provider. Therefore external story discovery is unavailable until a real provider is integrated. The educational news seed cards were intentionally removed because their source claims and timestamps were not independently evidenced. Empty is safer than fabricated.
